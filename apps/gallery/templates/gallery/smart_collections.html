How Smart Collections Work

Our AI analyzes your images to automatically create intelligent collections based on content, objects, scenes, and emotional sentiment.

AI Tag Analysis

Automatically detect and categorize images based on AI-generated tags like "meeting", "celebration", "team", and more.

Object Detection

Identify objects in images such as people, laptops, trophies, decorations, and group them accordingly.

Sentiment Analysis

Analyze the emotional tone of images to create collections based on positive, professional, celebratory sentiments.

Auto Updates

Collections automatically update when new images are added, ensuring your galleries stay organized without manual work.
