Who We Are

Your Trusted Partner in Community Development

Established with a vision to uplift local communities in Rupa Rural Municipality, Bhanjyang Cooperative provides accessible and fair financial solutions. We believe in strengthening the local economy by empowering individuals and businesses through savings, credit, and expert financial guidance.

Our commitment goes beyond traditional banking; we are deeply rooted in the community, striving to make a tangible difference in the lives of our members.
