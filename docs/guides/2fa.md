# Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) Guide

## Overview
Bhanjyang Cooperative uses Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) to secure the admin panel. This adds an extra layer of protection by requiring a time-sensitive code in addition to your password.

## Setting Up 2FA

1.  **Login**: Go to `/admin/`. Enter your username and password.
2.  **Prompt**: If you haven't set up 2FA, you will be redirected to a setup page.
3.  **Scan QR Code**:
    *   Open your authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, Authy, etc.).
    *   Select "Add Account" or "Scan QR Code".
    *   Scan the image displayed on the screen.
4.  **Verify**: Enter the 6-digit code generated by your app into the "Token" field on the website.
5.  **Backup Codes**:
    *   **Crucial Step**: You will be shown a list of backup codes.
    *   **Save These**: Copy/Print these codes and store them safely. They are the *only* way to access your account if you lose your phone.

## Logging In

1.  Enter Username/Password.
2.  Enter the 6-digit code from your app when prompted.

## Troubleshooting

*   **Lost Phone**: Use one of your backup codes to log in. Then go to your user profile in Admin to reset your 2FA device.
*   **Time Sync**: Ensure your phone's time is set to "Automatic". 2FA codes are time-based and will fail if your clock is off.
